Built for the way a backlog actually works

Not a spreadsheet with a coat of paint. The things you'd otherwise keep in your head, or a notes app, or nowhere.

Sessions that time themselves

Start a session from the app, a widget, your watch, or the Lock Screen. The Live Activity keeps counting while you play, and nudges you if you forget to stop it.

Trackers per game

Bosses, collectibles, upgrades, endings. Use a built-in tracker, write your own goals, or let AI draft one from a guide — then check things off as you go.

Playthroughs and runs

Separate progress for a second run or NG+. Roguelikes get their own record: loadout, outcome, win rate over time.

Collections and shelves

Group a trilogy, a comfort-games list, or everything you own on Genesis. Browse by system with icons that look like the hardware.

Stats worth reading

Hours by game, ratings, completions by year, and what you actually finished versus what you meant to.

Yours, privately

Everything lives on your device and in your own iCloud. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking — and you can export the whole library whenever you want.

Sorted by system

Your library grouped by the hardware it lives on — original console first, emulators last.
