Roadmap

Last reviewed 17 August 2026

This is a direction, not a set of promises. Things move between sections, and some never ship at all. For what has actually shipped, see the changelog.

Now

Being worked on.

  • Settling the beta. Making the things people use every day dependable before adding more.
  • Trackers you can trust. Generating a tracker should never be a gamble with progress you've already made.
  • Mac polish. The Mac app works, but it doesn't yet feel as considered as the iPhone one.

Next

Planned, not started.

  • Build a tracker piece by piece. Say what you actually care about — trophies, bosses, collectibles — and add categories one at a time instead of waiting on one big generation that can fail entirely.
  • Moments worth celebrating. Finishing a game, hitting a milestone, or completing a hundred small things should feel like something.
  • Replays. A look back at what you played over a month, a quarter or a year, built entirely from your own library on your own device.
  • Deeper stats. More ways to see your own playing habits — by year, by genre, by system.
  • A wider beta. Opening things up beyond the current small group.

Exploring

Ideas being thought about. No commitment, and some of these are genuinely hard.

  • Badges. Recognition for what you've actually done, earned once and kept.
  • Your own cover art. Swap a game's cover for something you prefer.
  • Scanning a written list. Point a camera at a checklist you wrote by hand and turn it into a tracker.
  • More ways to bring a library in, beyond CSV.
  • Making the app feel more like your own notebook — themes, icons, and the sort of personality a paper journal has.

Not planned

Worth being clear about, since these are what the app is deliberately not:

  • Accounts. Your library lives in your own iCloud. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • Ads, analytics or tracking. Not now, not later. See the privacy policy.
  • A social network. Sharing something you choose to share is one thing; a feed is another.
