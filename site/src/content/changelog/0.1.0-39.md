Your library already lived on your consoles. Build 39 goes and gets it.

### Steam, PlayStation and Xbox [new]

**Settings → Steam, PlayStation, or Xbox.** Each one reads what you have played there and brings it in for review, the way a CSV import does — nothing is added until you have looked.

- **Xbox** signs in on Microsoft's own page. LevelSelect never sees your password.
- **PlayStation** uses the same sign-in as Sony's PlayStation App: paste the NPSSO code from playstation.com once, and the app keeps only the sign-in it trades it for. Sony doesn't publish this route for other apps, so it could stop working without warning.
- **Steam** reads your library with the Web API key you registered on Steam. Steam only answers when your profile's game details are public.

Every sign-in and key stays in your device's Keychain and talks to Sony, Microsoft or Valve directly — never through LevelSelect's servers, and never synced. Steam and PlayStation playtime lands on the game as its own playthrough, and running the import again doesn't count it twice.

**Achievements come with them.** A game's page can import its Steam achievements, PlayStation trophies or Xbox achievements as a list, with what you have already unlocked ticked. Steam's lists don't need your key at all.

### An import that knows which game you mean [new]

**Pick the right game, by its box.** Tap the arrows on any row and choose from the games IGDB found — each with its cover, its release date on your console, and what else it came out on. Press and hold one to see the box at full size. *Modern Warfare 3* (2011, Xbox 360) and *Modern Warfare III* (2023, Xbox Series) differ by one numeral; now they look it. A search box finds anything the title didn't.

**It starts on the right one.** A name with no exact match starts on the game from the console the service reported, not whatever IGDB ranks first. "Brink™" is *Brink*, not *Brink of Consciousness* — trademark signs and punctuation no longer stop a match.

**The console you played it on.** Xbox lists every machine a game runs on, so a 360 game also names the Series consoles. The import starts each game on the newest console you have, and the console under each row is a menu: tick one, or several. A game you have on the Switch and played on the 360 can say both.

- It offers the machines the game is on that you own, even ones the service didn't name — *Minecraft Dungeons* on the Switch, Mac for a Steam game, a PlayStation VR headset, or the Quest, Vive, Rift or Index you play Steam's VR games on. A Steam Deck or Steam Machine is offered on every Steam game and never assumed.
- **Console for all** sets every row that offers a console at once — Mac for everything that runs on it, say.

**Games you already have get the console added.** A game in your library comes in as "In your library — adds Xbox 360", and importing it adds that console and changes nothing else. Run an import again and it has nothing to show, rather than a sheet of games you already own.

**Companion apps start unticked.** Halo Waypoint and the Minecraft Launcher are listed as games; a row with no achievements and no exact match on IGDB waits for you to tick it.

**itch.io comes through the same review.** A game IGDB knows by exactly that name is matched to it; the rest come in as named, with itch.io's own cover.

### Maps beside the tracker [new]

The tracker, the map and the videos open side by side in any combination — tracker and map, tracker and video, all three with video above the map, or any one alone. On a wide screen the checklist takes the room it needs and the map takes the rest.

**Pin a List.** A category of pins — every shrine, every seed — is one list you place pin by pin, with a count of how many are placed and Undo for the last one. Each list can have its own pin icon.

**Found is per playthrough.** Mark a pin found and it's found in this run. Start a new playthrough and it's unfound again, drawn with a dashed ring that says you found it before.

**Counted items count.** Nine hundred Korok seeds are one row that counts up, not nine hundred rows nobody could scroll. Finding one seed's pin counts one seed, finding it twice counts it once, and two devices' finds add up instead of overwriting each other.

### Lists, generated the way you planned them [new]

**Regenerate This List…** replaces the list under one category and keeps its pin. **Update My Lists** refreshes the generated ones and leaves your pasted lists, Personal Goals and imported achievements alone. **Generate All Planned** fills the empty plans, in tracker order, and a regeneration is told the categories you already have, so it fills your plan instead of inventing its own.

### Also new

- **System names** — PC can be PC, Windows, Linux or Computer, and its settings row says what it covers. A console's own page offers its names when it has more than one.

### Fixed

- An import of more than sixty games ran into IGDB's limit, and every game past it came back with nothing to choose from. It waits its turn now, and says so.
- Connecting a different itch.io account kept landing you in the first one. Each sign-in starts fresh.
- Adding a plan's items with Add New left it sitting among the unfilled plans, and the summary claimed it had removed as many items as it added.
- Replacing a tracker dropped your planned categories.
- Renaming a console — Genesis to Mega Drive — emptied a smart collection or Home Screen widget saved on it. They remember the console now, not what it was called.
