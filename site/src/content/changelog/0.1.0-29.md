### RetroAchievements, visibly

- **Every imported achievement wears its badge.** The art RA's community drew for each achievement now sits on its row — full colour once you've earned it, RA's own greyed variant while you haven't, so nothing unearned ever looks earned. One switch in Appearance turns the art off if you prefer plain rows. Already-installed sets pick their badges up on the next refresh.
- **A trophy shelf in Stats.** Every set you've mastered or completed, as a wall of RA's game icons — gold border for hardcore mastery, because that distinction is RA's and the app keeps it honest. Tap any icon to open that set on RetroAchievements.
- **Doors out.** The set's page on RA from the tracker and from the game's link row, and your own profile from RetroAchievements settings — all in the in-app browser.
