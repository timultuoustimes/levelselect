### Sync you can trust

- **Two devices, one timer.** Start a session on your phone while one is already running on your tablet — or resume an old session on one while the other starts a new one — and the app now sorts it out the same way on every device: the timer you acted on most recently keeps running, the other one closes with exactly the time it earned. No doubled hours, no timer that stops itself while you're watching it, no minutes counted twice.
- **Nothing gets deleted on a hunch.** Earlier builds could decide a playthrough that looked empty was a sync artifact and remove it — even when it was a real playthrough another device had just created and was still filling in. That guessing is gone. When two devices genuinely disagree, both copies stay visible and you decide.
- **Progress merges instead of overwriting.** If the same objective gets ticked on two devices, the more recent action wins everywhere, an item you deliberately unticked stays unticked, and two different notes on the same item are kept together rather than one quietly replacing the other.
- **The Lock Screen keeps up.** Stopping a session on one device now takes down the Live Activity on the other, instead of leaving a timer running with buttons that do nothing.
- **Repairs happen when changes arrive**, not just when you switch apps — so a page you're already looking at updates on its own.

### Knowing what's actually happening

- **Real iCloud status in Settings.** Synced, syncing, signed out, or "iCloud is catching up" when Apple is rate-limiting your devices after a busy stretch — with the last successful sync time, so a slow moment never looks like lost data.
- **Detached sessions are shown, not hidden.** In rare cases iCloud can separate a recorded session from its game. The app no longer guesses where it belongs; it keeps the time and tells you it's there.
- **Tracker generation reports back.** Generation takes a minute or two and you usually walk away — now a banner tells you when it finishes or fails, wherever you are in the app, with a button to jump straight to the game.

### Trackers

- **Pasted and generated lists keep every item.** Three collectibles all called "Chest" stay three separate rows. Duplicate internal IDs — which used to make two rows share one checkmark — are cleaned up at every entry point: first generation, Replace, Add New Only, and pasted lists alike.
- **Imported and locked lists survive regeneration**, including when a generated tracker reuses their category name.
- **Progress percentages stay honest.** Adding a personal goal, replacing a tracker, or rescuing dropped items now updates every playthrough's progress — not just the one you're looking at.
- **Removals you'd notice are reported.** An item you'd written a note on, or revealed, now counts as work worth warning you about before a Replace takes it away.

### Sessions and history

- **Ending a forgotten session records when you actually stopped.** For a paused session, that's the pause — not a time invented from when you started.
- **Clock differences between devices can't create nonsense.** No negative durations, no sessions recorded as ending in the future.

### Smaller things [improved]

- The tracker page shows which **game** you're in, not just the playthrough name.
- Add Game keeps **"Add manually"** reachable when a search fails or finds nothing.
- Rotating an iPad into the side-by-side layout no longer strands you on a duplicate tracker page.
- The "still playing?" prompt dismisses itself if you already answered it on another device, and its six-hour reminder can now break through silent mode.
- Export includes a stable ID for every record type it carries, and the app's description of it matches what the file really is.
