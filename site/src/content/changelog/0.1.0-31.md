### A finish that says how long it took

- **Start dates on beaten records.** A finish held one date, which made a six-week playthrough and a single evening look identical. Add a start beside it — day, month, or just the year, the same as the finish — and the record reads *Dec 2025 → Jan 2026*. Leave it out and nothing changes; most finishes logged from memory only know their end.

### Your words

- **Name the stars.** Settings → Appearance → Star names. "Hated it" through "Loved it" are only defaults; if three stars means *comfort game* to you, say so. Blank any one of them and the built-in word comes back. The names follow you to your other devices and show up in Stats.
- **Two more page backgrounds** — your accent colour, or plain — beside cover art and status colour.

### Your covers

- **Change Cover** (⋯ menu). IGDB usually has several covers and a pile of artwork per game; now you can pick one, or paste a URL to art of your own. The fetched cover is kept, untouched, so *Use fetched cover* is always one tap away — and a metadata refresh or a Fix Match can never overwrite the one you chose.
- **Media.** Screenshots for the game, from IGDB, on the game page.

### The page, arranged how you read it

- **Arrange Sections** (⋯ menu). Drag the game page's sections into the order you want and switch off the ones you don't use — the same treatment the Stats tab already had. Hiding is not deleting: a hidden Notes section still holds every note, and turning it back on brings them with it.
- **Collapsing a section is now per game.** It was library-wide by accident: collapsing Tracker on one game quietly collapsed it on all of them. A game with a hundred objectives and a game with none can want different answers.

### The library you had, and the words for it

- **Previously Owned.** A fourth ownership chip for the copy you sold, traded, or lent to someone who moved away. A library that spans your life holds games you no longer hold, and the only honest options before were to pretend you still owned it or to delete the history.
- **Tag suggestions.** Start typing a tag and the ones you already use appear underneath. Left alone, a hand-typed vocabulary splits into `roguelike`, `rogue-like` and `Roguelike` inside a month, and once it splits the tags stop being worth filtering by.
- **Manage Tags** (Settings → Manage Tags). Rename a tag everywhere, merge two by renaming one onto the other, or remove one from every game. Each says how many games it will change before it changes them.
