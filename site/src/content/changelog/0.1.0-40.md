The celebration build. Everything you've recorded already says something about you; build 40 says it back.

### Badges [new]

**Thirty-two of them, from your library** — firsts, milestones, habits, collections and history. Your first timed session, your tenth game beaten, a month where you played every week, going back to a game you hadn't touched in six months, every game on one system beaten.

- **Earned once, kept for good.** A badge stays even if you later delete the game that earned it. It's a record of something you did, not a live count.
- **Dated from what earned it.** The first time you open this build, the library you already have earns its share quietly, each badge dated to when you actually did the thing, and the Badges page says so once.
- **The moment it lands.** A new badge arrives with a torch-colored glow, confetti over it, and a note at the bottom of the screen. It waits for any sheet or alert to close first. With Reduce Motion on, just the glow.
- **Journal → Badges** has them all, earned and not, and tapping one opens it big. There's a **Badges widget** in every size, including iOS 27's tall one on iPad and Mac.

### Replay [new]

**Journal → Charts, at the top.** A month, a quarter or a year of your play, read back to you in a sentence — "Mostly Hollow Knight", "One long Saturday with Balatro, and not much else" — with the box art of what you played most, what you finished, and the badges you earned.

- **Before you tracked.** Hours from Steam, PlayStation or Xbox get their own page, with the years you placed them in.
- **Save as image** makes a card for posting: the box art, the one line worth saying, the console, and LevelSelect on it.

### Imported hours, in the years they happened [new]

Steam and the consoles report a lifetime total and no dates. **Open a game and tap its time played** to say which year it belongs to, or a range of years — "I played Cities: Skylines most in 2021 to 2023." A range appears in full on each of those years' Replays rather than being split between them, and it's never added to any year's dated total, because it never happened on any one day.

### Console pages wear the console's logo [new]

The SNES in its red, the Genesis in its outline, the Dreamcast's swirl. A logo that would vanish on the ground — the PS5's black wordmark on the dark theme — is drawn in the text color instead, and only the parts that vanish. The logo follows the name you chose in **Settings → System names**, so a Mega Drive is a Mega Drive. Consoles with no logo keep their name. *Use game logos* in Settings turns these off too. The logos load straight from Wikimedia Commons, which is sent the logo's file name and nothing else; the [privacy policy](/privacy/) says so.

### Also new

- **Library: delete several games at once**, and Undo puts the whole batch back.
- **Memories: pick several pictures at once.**
- **Backups carry your badges** and every field this build added, so a restore puts them all back.
- **Discard a mis-tapped session.** Start and Stop by mistake, and the question that follows offers to drop anything under a minute.
- **Library gets out of the way.** The ownership chips scroll with your games until a filter is on. A small library doesn't get genre and theme rows until there's a shelf to browse.

### Fixed

- **Charts' Played total includes imported hours** and says how many, so it agrees with Most Played and the game page.
- **By Month starts at your first month**, rather than five empty ones above it.
- **Replay dates a session from when it started**, the same way Charts does.
- **A finish logged as "just the year" reads as that year**, never 1 January.
- **Years read as years** — "2018", not "2,018".
- **The largest text sizes**: Badges and Charts stack instead of cutting their numbers off.
- **Text fields** no longer draw black boxes on the purple ground.
- **A game with no tracker** says "no tracker yet" instead of 0%.
- **Durations** drop a trailing "0s".
- **Game pages** no longer show "Series: Q116947323" when Wikidata only has an id.
- **Search** opens a game you tap in its results every time, and a game page's tracker, genres and system open from Search, News and Wishlist.
