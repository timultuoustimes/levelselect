Another beta. Most of this build is about trackers: getting a list in, fixing one row without redoing the rest, and reaching it from the Home Screen.

### Some widgets need adding again [changed]

**Continue Playing and sixteen other widgets changed how they are configured**, so that settings can be added to them later without breaking anything. The cost is paid once, now: a copy already on your Home or Lock Screen may freeze on a placeholder after updating. Remove it and add it again.

- **The Badges widget is gone for now.** Your badges are untouched, in Journal → Badges.
- **Continue Playing** keeps its small and medium sizes. Edit Widget lets you choose game art or your latest screenshot for the picture, and whether to show the other games you're playing.

### Command Board, on iPhone [new]

**On iOS 27, a tall widget that takes a whole Home Screen page.** One game, with Start Session and Open Playthrough, two of its tracker lists, and a shelf of other games underneath.

- **Tap a card to tick it off.** A card that counts has − and +; tapping the rest of that card opens its list in the app. A change can take a few seconds to show on the widget.
- **The arrows beside each list** page through what is left to do. View More opens the tracker.
- **Edit Widget** chooses the game, which two lists it shows, and the shelf: Also Playing, Backlog, Up Next, Always Around, Shelved or Completed. Left alone, it shows the game you played last and Also Playing. View More on the shelf opens that shelf's full list.

iPad keeps its own extra-large Command Board.

### Spreadsheets [new]

In a game's tracker, tap **Paste**. Paste a Google Sheets link and tap **Read**, or copy cells out of a sheet and paste them. Copied cells are read as a table now, the same way a link is.

- **Review Import accounts for the sheet.** How many rows it read, which rows and columns it left out, which tab it read and how many others there are, and whether the rows are new or already on your tracker.
- **Say what each column is.** Tap the columns line: the name, where it is, a description, the tick, or a new column of your own (text, a number, on/off, or a choice). The app's own guess is the starting point.
- **Two columns that could both be the tick means neither is used** until you choose one.
- **Rows already on your tracker are not duplicated or overwritten.** A tick or a value from the sheet only fills in where the row has none.
- **Planners, run logs and calculators are turned away** rather than turned into a checklist.

**What it does not do.** Only the first table on a tab is read. Values come in as they read now: a number the sheet calculates will not recalculate in LevelSelect, and formulas and formatting are not kept. A sheet read by link has to be shared with "Anyone with the link"; otherwise copy the cells and paste. If a sheet is turned away and you think it should not have been, there is no way to override that yet. **Didn't come out right? Send it to LevelSelect** on the same screen opens an email with the sheet attached, and nothing is sent until you tap Send.

### Trackers you can build and correct by hand [new]

- **Write your own** builds a whole tracker: name a list, type its rows, add another list.
- **Add…** at the foot of a list adds a row. Imported achievement sets don't have it, because those lists are the developer's.
- **Delete one row** from its menu, instead of deleting the list it is in.
- **Move Up, Move Down, and Move to… another list.** A moved row is the same row, so its tick, note and values go with it. Moving between a list remembered across playthroughs and one that isn't asks first, because the tick is read from a different place.
- **A row's description can be edited**, so one wrong line in a generated list is a correction, not a regeneration.
- **Generated trackers read before they write.** The generator reads the game's wiki, and its Steam achievement list where there is one, so names and counts should match the game.
- **Built from**, at the foot of a tracker, links the pages it was built from.

### Achievement art from every service [new]

Achievements and trophies imported from **Steam, PlayStation and Xbox** show their own icons in the tracker, as RetroAchievements already did: gray until you tick one off, in color after. Lists you imported before this build should show them without importing again. To hide them, turn off **Show achievement badges** in Settings.

The sheets where you pick a game to import from now show each game's art and an **Add** button, and the first result is labeled **Best match**.

### Runs [new]

- **Pause Run and Resume Run** sit beside End Run. Paused time is left out of the run's length.
- **Discard Run**, when ending a run, drops the attempt instead of saving it.
- **Choosing several options** on a run: the whole row is the tap target, and every option shows whether it is selected.

### Also new

- **Next up follows where you left off.** Widgets that show your next objective lead with the list you last ticked something in.
- **Your own cover art reaches widgets.** A photo or screenshot you chose as a game's cover shows there instead of a placeholder.
- **Export game list (CSV)** in Settings → Import & Export, for taking your list to a spreadsheet or another app. The full JSON export is still the one that restores.

### Fixed

- **Shelved no longer becomes the default status** on Add Game after you use it once.
- **Imported hours belong to no year until you say which.** Hours from Steam, PlayStation or Xbox were being placed in the year you connected the account.
