### The game you haven't opened in three weeks

- **Where you left off.** The Tracker section now leads with the last thing you ticked, when you ticked it, and your note about it if you left one. A percentage tells you how far you got; it can't tell you what you were in the middle of. Coming back to a save after a month shouldn't feel like reading someone else's file.
- **Home says it too.** The Continue Playing card told you how long ago and for how long, never what you were doing — the actual question you opened the app with. It now carries the last thing you ticked, and so does the Continue Playing widget, so the answer is on your Home Screen before you open anything.
- **Notes say their own name.** Any tracker item can carry a note of your own — which was true before, and unfindable, because it lived behind a press-and-hold menu item called "Edit". The menu now offers **Add a Note** (or *Edit Your Note*) outright, alongside *Rename, Location &amp; Note*. The docs explain it too, which they never did.

### Finishes you can correct

- **Edit a beaten record.** Tap any entry under Beaten to fix a date you now remember better, change what it was, or add detail. Previously a finish could only be deleted and re-entered.
- **Played with.** Record who was there — as many people as were, each with a name, a gamertag, or both. It goes on finishes, sessions and runs, because co-op is mostly an ordinary evening rather than the credits. After the first time, the app offers the people you've played with before: one tap puts the name and handle back exactly as you had them. It's written down and nothing more — no accounts, nothing shared, nothing to sign into.
- **A playthrough can say how it ended** — finished, dropped, shelved, or the save was lost — with the sentence explaining why, which is the part worth keeping. Status lives on the game, so until now the save you abandoned looked identical to the one you love. Nothing is ever assumed from inactivity.
- **Hints, per game.** Play one game blind without giving up hints everywhere else (⋯ → Item Hints).

### Small things [improved]

- **Runs and Beaten stop sharing an icon.** The checkered flag belongs to Beaten — it's the finish line. Runs takes the repeat glyph, which is what a run actually is: another attempt.
- **The tracker mentions notes once**, if you have items and haven't written one, and stops mentioning them the moment you do.
