### The header

- **The art is the page now.** The backdrop used to sit behind the game's name, which meant it had to be blurred into a wash so the words stayed readable — you could change the image and not be able to tell. Everything that has to be *read* moved onto a translucent panel in front, so the art holds full strength, runs under the navigation bar, and scrolls away with the header instead of sitting under the section list.
- **Your game's logo, where its name was.** A wordmark says *Hades* faster than the word does. Logos come from IGDB and SteamGridDB automatically; the name renders as text when there's no logo, when you turn it off, and always at large text sizes — a logo is a picture of text and can't grow with Dynamic Type.
- **Key art without a logo is preferred for the backdrop**, now that the page draws the logo itself. Otherwise the name appeared twice, at whatever size the publisher chose.

### The numbers, back where you can see them

- **A stats row on every game page:** played, sessions, beaten, and runs on games that log them. The web app had this and the native app lost it — the figures were inside the Sessions section, so collapsing a long list of sittings took a game's total playtime with it.
- **Lifetime totals, across every playthrough.** The old number described one playthrough, so starting a second made the figure on screen quietly smaller than the truth. *How long have I spent on this?* has never meant *since the most recent restart*.
- **Switch the row off if you don't time your play.** Settings → Game pages & trackers → Arrange game pages. A permanent "0s played" is a reproach to someone who just wanted to catalogue a shelf.

### Artwork has roles

- **Cover, logo, and backdrop are three separate slots**, each with its own picker, each fillable from IGDB, from SteamGridDB, from your own photos or files, or from a pasted link.
- **SteamGridDB joins IGDB.** IGDB's art is the publisher's and stays first; SteamGridDB fills the gaps, which for logos is most of the catalogue. No account, no key, nothing to set up.
- **Your own images sync.** A photo of the box you actually owned is a legitimate cover.

### The ⋯ menu is seven things

- **It was fifteen**, ordered by whichever build added them, and long enough to scroll. Opening it no longer answered a stable question.
- **Everything else moved to the surface it affects.** Run logging sits in the Runs section; tracker layout and hints sit in the Tracker section they change; arranging game pages moved to Settings, where a library-wide preference belongs instead of masquerading as an action on one game.
- **One vocabulary for status.** The game menu said *Playing* and *Queued* while every shelf, filter and setting beside it said *Now Playing* and *Up Next*. Status is a central idea here; it doesn't get to change names depending on which screen you're on.
- **Deleting a game asks first, everywhere.** The same delete was confirmed and explained from the page and instant from a long press, so what "Delete" meant depended on where you were holding.

### Smaller things

- Rating labels, colours and page background reset **independently** now, so fixing a backdrop no longer throws away five rating labels you wrote by hand.
- Console names use their short forms in the header — *SNES*, not *Super Nintendo Entertainment System* wrapped over two lines.
- The ownership chips stop wrapping awkwardly on narrower phones.
- Fixed: the colour picker closed Settings the moment you touched a colour, so a colour could be stabbed at but never adjusted.
- Fixed: on iPad in landscape, the header art sat below the bars with an empty band above it.
