Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026

LevelSelect has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your library is stored on your device and synced to your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot read it.

What stays on your device

Your games, play sessions, tracker progress, playthroughs, runs, collections, ratings, notes, and linked videos are stored locally and synced through your private iCloud database using CloudKit. That data is under your Apple account's control. Deleting the app and its iCloud data removes it. You can export everything to a file at any time from Settings.

What leaves your device

Three features talk to LevelSelect's backend. In each case, only what's listed is sent:

FeatureWhat is sentWhere it goes
Game searchThe search text or game id you look upOur proxy → IGDB, for titles, cover art, and release dates
AI tracker generationThe game's name, its public IGDB metadata, and any guide text or URL you provideOur generator → Anthropic's Claude, which may search the web for a guide
Map search (planned)The game's name and an optional wiki page URLOur finder → Anthropic's Claude with web search

Separately, and entirely from your device: your public Deku Deals wishlist is fetched if you configure one, cover art loads from IGDB's image servers, and YouTube titles and thumbnails load for links you add.

The install identifier

Requests to our backend carry a random identifier the app generates on first launch, used only to enforce fair-use rate limits. It isn't derived from your device's hardware, isn't linked to you or your iCloud account, and resets if you delete and reinstall the app.

What LevelSelect never does

  • No user accounts, sign-ins, or passwords
  • No advertising, and no data sold or shared with data brokers
  • No analytics or telemetry SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera

Crash reports and TestFlight

During the beta, Apple's TestFlight may share crash logs and any feedback you choose to submit, governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

LevelSelect is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted here with a new date, and material changes are noted in the release notes.

Contact

Questions or concerns: privacy@levelselect.app, or use TestFlight's built-in feedback during the beta.
